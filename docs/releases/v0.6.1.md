Kuberniva 0.6.1 brings object details into the Topology map, keeps live data fresh when you come back to the window, and fixes two pages that would not scroll.

## ✨ What's new

**Topology details**
- Clicking an object on the **Topology** map now opens its details in a popup over the map instead of leaving the page
- **Overview** shows the essentials for each kind: Pod node, IP, and containers with their state and restarts; replicas and images for workloads; ports and selector for Services; rules for Ingresses; capacity for volume claims; and key names for ConfigMaps and Secrets
- **Connections** lists what the object routes to, selects, owns, and uses, and what uses it; click one to jump to it
- **YAML** and **Events** tabs, plus **Logs** and **Shell** for Pods and **Open in Workloads** or **Resources** when you want the full view
- The selected object is outlined on the map, its status follows the 15-second refresh, and the popup says so if the object is deleted while you look at it
- Secret values are never shown, only their key names

**Fresh data after time away**
- When you return to Kuberniva after a minute or more in the background (another app, Stage Manager, or minimized), it quietly reconnects to the cluster with fresh connections and credentials
- Every live list and feed resumes from exactly where it stopped, so only what changed while you were away arrives, and nothing on screen resets
- Pods deleted while you were away disappear before you click them, and logs and shells open on a working connection

## 🛠 Fixes

- The **Events** page could not scroll, so older events were cut off
- **Settings** could not scroll, which hid its lower sections when release notes were showing

## 🔄 Updating

- **In the app:** **Kuberniva → Check for Updates…**, or **Settings → Check for updates → Download and install → Restart now**
- **With Homebrew:** `brew upgrade --cask --greedy kuberniva`
- **New install:** `brew install --cask velqa/tap/kuberniva`, or download the DMG below

Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
