Kuberniva 0.6.2 streams logs live, keeps table columns in proportion as the window changes, and makes the interface calmer and livelier.

## ✨ What's new

**Live log streaming**
- Pod logs now stream as they are written, like `kubectl logs -f`, instead of refreshing every 30 seconds
- The log toolbar shows a pulsing **Streaming** dot while lines arrive, **Connecting…** while it reconnects, and **Container stopped** when a finished container will write no more
- Dropped connections reconnect on their own and continue after the last line shown, with no repeated or missing lines
- **Refresh** and switching containers or Pods restart the stream from the latest lines

**Columns that follow the window**
- After you resize a column, every column keeps its share of the table: make the window wider or narrower and the columns stretch or shrink together, keeping their shape
- Columns never get narrower than their minimum; below that, the table scrolls sideways
- Dragging still follows the pointer exactly, and double-clicking a column edge resets the whole table
- Applies to Pods and other workloads, Resources, the Overview's nodes, Argo CD, Helm, and the OCM hub

**Sidebar motion**
- Items in the sidebar lift slightly with an accent bar as you move over them, and their icons pop
- A newly selected page springs into place, in both the main list and the Resources trees
- Respects the macOS **Reduce motion** setting

**Less clutter**
- Removed explanatory captions under page headers and actions (Events, Helm, Topology, Workloads, Pod details, the resource directories, and Settings), since the pages speak for themselves
- Messages no longer repeat the app's name

## 🔄 Updating

- **In the app:** **Kuberniva → Check for Updates…**, or **Settings → Check for updates → Download and install → Restart now**
- **With Homebrew:** `brew upgrade --cask --greedy kuberniva`
- **New install:** `brew install --cask velqa/tap/kuberniva`, or download the DMG below

Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
